How to play

Like a puzzle book: every row, column and 3×3 box holds 1 to 9 once.

Pencil: tap
Tap a square, then numbers, to note them small. Or tap a number (every copy lights up), then squares. Tap a note again to take it out. Notes are never checked.
Pen: drag
Drag a number onto a square to ink it. Ink is your answer and stays. Nothing is checked until the grid is full; then you hear whether it's solved, or how many squares are wrong.
Erase and White-out
Erase clears the pencil notes in a square. White-out covers ink with a white patch that stays, so you can ink again. Solved puzzles are final.
Hint and Look
The back of the book. Hint circles any wrong ink (then wait 2 minutes for the next). Look opens the answer key. Both are always there, and both are noted on the puzzle. Solve without them and it counts as on your own.
The clock
Counts only while the board is in front of you. It stops for these rules, Settings, the answer key, and when you leave.

How to play

A crossword of sums: every sum across and down must come out true. √ and ² go first, then × and ÷, then + and −.

Pencil: tap
Tap an empty box, then numbers in the bank, to note them small. Or tap a number, then boxes. Tap a note again to take it out. Notes are never checked.
Pen: drag
Drag a number from the bank onto a box to ink it. Each bank number goes in once. Nothing is checked until every box is full; then you hear whether it's solved, or how many boxes are wrong.
Erase and White-out
Erase clears the pencil notes in a box. White-out covers ink with a white patch that stays and gives the number back to the bank. Solved puzzles are final.
Hint and Look
The back of the book. Hint circles any wrong ink (then wait 2 minutes for the next). Look opens the answer key. Both are always there, and both are noted on the puzzle. Solve without them and it counts as on your own.
Moving around
Pinch to zoom in, drag the puzzle to move it. Tap a box to light up its sums.
The clock
Counts only while the puzzle is in front of you. It stops for these rules, Settings, the answer key, and when you leave.
